Privacy policy

What we collect when you report food, why we collect it, and who can see it.

What we collect

  • Your full name, email address and mobile number
  • Your barangay and the pickup location
  • Food details: what it is, how much, the best-before date, its condition and a short description
  • A photo of the food, if you choose to add one. This demo keeps only the photo's file name.

Why we collect it

We use it to check that the food is safe, arrange the pickup, and let you follow your report with its reference number. We do not use it for anything else.

Who can see it

Only barangay staff can see your name, email and mobile number. They are never shown on the Home page or the Track page. Anyone with a reference number can see the food, its status and the staff remarks, but not who reported it.

Your rights under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173)

The Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) protects your personal information. Under this law you may:

  • Be told how your information is used
  • Ask to see the information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct it or remove it
  • Object to how it is used
  • Complain to the National Privacy Commission if you think your rights were not respected

To use any of these rights, contact the barangay.

This is a class demo

Barangay Bagsakan is a school project for Web Development (WEBDEVT). There is no server and no database. Everything you enter is saved only in your own browser, on your own device, and nothing is sent anywhere. You can remove it any time by clearing your browser's site data.

In short

  • We ask only for what staff need to verify and collect the food.
  • Only barangay staff see your name and contact details.
  • Public pages never show who reported the food.
  • In this demo, your data stays in your browser.
