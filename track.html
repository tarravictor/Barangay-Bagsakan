    What each status means

    1. Reported. Your report was received. Staff will check it soon.
    2. Verified. Staff checked that the food is safe to share. Volunteers are arranging pickup.
    3. Picked up. The food was collected and delivered to people who need it.

    If you see "Requires more info"

    Staff need something from you, such as a clearer photo. Read the staff remarks, then log in and use Reply to staff on this page.

    If you see "Rejected"

    The food can't be shared, for example because it is past its best-before date. The staff remarks explain why.

    If you see "Duplicate"

    This food was already reported. The staff remarks give the first report's reference number.
