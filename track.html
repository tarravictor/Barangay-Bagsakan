    What each status means

    1. Submitted. Your report was sent.
    2. Received. Barangay staff have it in their queue.
    3. Under Review. Staff are checking that the food is safe.
    4. In Progress. Verified. Volunteers are arranging pickup.
    5. Resolved. The food was picked up and delivered.

    If you see "Requires More Info"

    Staff need something from you, such as a clearer photo. Read the remarks, then use Update report.
