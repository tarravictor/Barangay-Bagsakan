Log in

Donors log in to report surplus food and follow their reports. Barangay staff log in to manage reports.

No account yet? Register as a donor

Why do I need an account?

Only registered donors can report food. This keeps out spam and fake reports, and lets staff contact you about pickup.

Barangay staff

Log in with the barangay admin account to open the dashboard, where you can review reports and change their status.

Demo note

Accounts are saved in this browser only. Passwords are stored as a SHA-256 hash, never as typed.
