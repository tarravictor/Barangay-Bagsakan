Share food, not waste.

Barangay Bagsakan lets you report surplus edible food in our barangay and helps connect it with neighbors and organizations who can still use it. Barangay staff verify every report before pickup.

How it works

From your kitchen to a neighbor's table in three steps.

  1. 1

    Reported

    You submit your surplus food report with quantity, expiration date and pickup location.

  2. 2

    Verified

    Barangay staff review the report and check that the food is suitable for redistribution.

  3. 3

    Picked up

    Volunteers collect the food and deliver it to people in need.

Frequently asked questions

What to report, and what happens after you do.

See all FAQs

Edible, safe food such as fresh fruits and vegetables, bread and pastries, packaged goods that are not yet expired, and properly stored cooked meals. Spoiled or expired food should not be reported.

After barangay staff review your report, its status changes to Verified. You can check it anytime on the Track a report page.

Use the reference number you received after submitting. Enter it on the Track a report page to see the current status and latest update.
