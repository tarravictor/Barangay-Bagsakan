What can I donate?

Expired, spoiled, or opened food. Staff check every report before pickup.

Cooked meals, fruits and vegetables, bread and pastries, rice and grains, canned and packaged goods, dairy and eggs, meat and seafood, and other edible surplus food.

Reporting

Click Report Surplus Food, fill in the food details, expiry date, and pickup address, add a photo, confirm the food is safe and not expired, then submit your report.

Yes. The expiry date and the safety confirmation are required so unusable food never reaches staff.

A photo is optional, but it helps staff check your food faster. Use one JPG, PNG, or WEBP file, up to 5 MB.

Tracking

Open the Track page and enter the reference number you received after submitting, for example BB-2026-001.

Barangay staff received your report and are checking the food details and safety before arranging pickup.

The status changes to Rejected and the reason is shown on your report. You will be notified, and you may correct the information and submit a new report.

Staff need something more from you, such as a clearer photo. Click Update report, add the missing information, and the status goes back to Under Review.

Pickup and safety

Authorized barangay staff review every report and verify that the food is safe before pickup is arranged.

Barangay staff coordinate the collection and connect the food with families or partner organizations who need it.

Privacy

Your name, email, and contact number are used only to process your report and send you status updates.
