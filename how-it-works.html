How it works

From your kitchen to a family that needs it, in four steps. Every report is checked by barangay staff before any food is shared.

The four steps

  1. 1. Register

    Create a free donor account with your name, email and mobile number. Only registered donors can report, which keeps out spam and fake reports.

    Register as a donor

  2. 2. Report the food

    Tell us what the food is, how much there is, its condition, its best-before date and where to pick it up. Add one clear photo. You get a reference number right away.

    Report surplus food

  3. 3. Staff check it

    Barangay staff look at the details and the photo. If the food is safe to share, they mark it Verified and arrange a pickup. If they need something from you, they mark it Requires more info.

  4. 4. Pickup

    Volunteers collect the food and bring it to families or the community kitchen. The report changes to Picked up.

    Track a report

What each status means

Reported
We received your report. Staff will check it soon.
Verified
Staff checked the food and are arranging pickup.
Picked up
The food was collected and shared. Thank you!
Requires more info
Staff need something from you, like a clearer photo. Read their remarks on the Track page and send a reply there. Your report then goes back to Reported for another check.
Rejected
The food can't be shared, for example because it is past its best-before date. The remarks explain why.
Duplicate
The same food was already reported. The remarks give the reference number of the first report.

Reporting guidelines

Please report

  • Cooked food that was kept covered and at a safe temperature
  • Fresh fruits, vegetables, bread and rice that are still good
  • Sealed canned and packaged goods before their best-before date
  • Only food you have the right to give away

Please do not report

  • Spoiled, expired or moldy food
  • Opened or partly eaten items
  • Food that was not stored safely
  • The same food twice. The site blocks duplicate reports and shows you the first reference number.

Reports with false information may be rejected, and staff may contact you about them.
