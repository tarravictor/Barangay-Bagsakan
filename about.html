About Barangay Bagsakan

A simple way for neighbors to report surplus food, so it gets shared instead of thrown away.

Our mission

Share food, not waste.

Barangay Bagsakan helps residents report surplus edible food from homes, carinderias, stores, markets and events. Barangay staff check every report, and volunteers bring safe food to neighbors who can still use it.

The problem

Good food gets thrown out while neighbors go without.

After a fiesta, a busy market day or a slow day at the carinderia, edible food is often left over. Most people want to give it away, but they do not know who to call or how to make sure it is safe.

So the food is thrown out, even though someone nearby could have eaten it. Barangay Bagsakan gives residents one clear place to report it and one reference number to follow.

How it works

Three steps from leftover food to a neighbor's table.

  1. 1

    Reported

    You report the food with its quantity, best-before date and pickup location. You get a reference number.

  2. 2

    Verified

    Barangay staff check the report and make sure the food is safe to share.

  3. 3

    Picked up

    Volunteers collect the food and deliver it to people who need it.

You can follow your report any time on the Track a report page.
